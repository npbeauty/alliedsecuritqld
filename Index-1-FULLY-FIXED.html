Privacy Policy — Allied Security QLD

Effective Date: 2026 — Australia Wide Operations — Brisbane HQ

1. What Makes Security Company Privacy Different?
Unlike a normal retail company, a security company collects sensitive security data: site addresses, access codes, guard licence numbers, client risk assessments, incident reports, CCTV footage. Under Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) & QLD Information Privacy Act, this is classified as high-risk personal information and requires extra protection, secure encrypted storage, and limited access (Security principle).

2. Data We Collect for Quotes & Payments
• Client: Full name/company, ABN, email, phone, site address, service type, date/time, number of guards, budget, payment method
• Payment: Card handled by Stripe/PayPal (we never store full card numbers), bank details for invoice, billing address
• Guard applicants: Security licence, RSA, First Aid, Right to Work, ID documents (stored in Supabase private bucket guard-documents with RLS, not public)
• Incident data: If an incident occurs on your site, we may collect incident reports, photos, timesheets — only shared with you and authorities if required by law.

3. How We Use & Store
• To generate automated quotes with zone-adjusted rates (Brisbane base, Sydney 1.15x etc), detect public holidays, calculate day 6am-6pm / night 6pm-6am split, and enforce 4hr minimum
• To roster licensed guards matching your city and service (Static, Crowd Controller, Retail, Construction, Event, Bodyguard, Armed)
• To process payment (card, bank transfer, 50% deposit)
• Storage: Supabase Australia region, private encrypted bucket, RLS enabled, access limited to admin only. Guard documents are never public.
• Retention: Quotes 7 years (ATO requirement), guard applications until expiry + 1 year, incident reports 7 years.

4. Security-Specific Protections (Not in Normal Companies)
• Site Confidentiality: Your site address and security vulnerabilities are never shared with other clients or guards not rostered to you.
• Guard Verification: All guards QLD verified, licence checked via OFS, RSA & First Aid validated.
• No Public Listing: We never publish client list or site locations.
• Incident Confidentiality: Incident reports shared only with client, insurer, and police if legally required.
• Subcontractor Control: For national jobs (Sydney, Melbourne, Perth), vetted subcontractors sign NDA and must hold valid Master Licence for that state.

5. Your Rights
Access, correction, deletion request via admin@alliedsecurityqld.com.au. We respond within 7 days. You can opt-out of marketing but not of security compliance notices.

6. Payment Privacy
Card payments processed by Stripe (PCI DSS compliant). We see only last 4 digits and receipt. Bank transfer details stored for invoicing only. No card data stored on our servers.

Contact: Allied Security QLD — Gold Coast Base, Australia Wide. For privacy complaints: OAIC - oaic.gov.au

Terms & Conditions — Quote, Booking & Payment — Allied Security QLD

Effective: 2026 — National Rates — Brisbane HQ

1. Security Company Terms — Why Different From Normal Business?
Unlike retail or cleaning, security services are governed by Security Providers Act QLD, Security Services Industry Award 2020, Work Health & Safety Act, and State Master Licence conditions. These terms override normal consumer terms: minimum shift lengths, licence requirements, right to refuse unsafe sites, and incident reporting obligations.

2. Quote & Rates (Automated System)
• Base rates are Brisbane ex GST, zone-adjusted: Sydney 1.15x, Melbourne 1.12x, Gold Coast 1.05x, Perth 1.10x, Darwin 1.13x, Adelaide/Newcastle/Sunshine Coast 0.95x, Regional (Toowoomba etc) 0.90x + travel.
• Day Rate 06:00-18:00, Night Rate 18:00-06:00 — shifts crossing 6pm are automatically split (e.g. 4pm-10pm = 2h day + 4h night).
• Public Holiday rates override weekend rates. PH list includes New Year, Australia Day, Good Friday, Easter Sat/Sun/Mon, ANZAC Day, Labour Day QLD, King's Birthday, Ekka Brisbane, Christmas/Boxing Day + custom festival dates you add.
• Quote valid 14 days. Rates exclude GST unless stated inc GST. Award compliant — we must pay guards at least $28.42/hr base + penalties per Security Services Award.

3. Minimum Hours — Very Important (Security Industry Standard)
• 4 hour minimum metro (Brisbane, Gold Coast, Sydney, Melbourne, Perth, Canberra)
• 6 hour minimum regional & public holidays
• 6-8 hour minimum for Bodyguard / Armed Security
If client selects less than minimum (e.g. 2 hours), system automatically charges 4 hours. This is industry standard and required to cover guard travel, licence, insurance. Client agrees to this by ticking checkbox.

4. Booking, Cancellation & Emergency
• Standard cancellation: 24 hours notice, otherwise full shift charged.
• Public Holiday / Events / Bodyguard / Armed: 48 hours notice, otherwise full shift charged.
• Emergency <24hr booking: +25% loading (covers overtime, rapid rostering).
• Client must provide safe site, lighting, amenities, and accurate risk info. We reserve right to withdraw guard if site unsafe (WHS Act).

5. Payment Terms
• Options: Card (instant), Bank Transfer (7 days), 50% Deposit (balance on completion).
• Late payment: 2% per month + costs of recovery.
• Deposit secures guards for booked dates. No refund if cancelled within notice period.
• Travel: $0.95/km + $35-75 travel allowance if >40km from base city. Accommodation at cost for regional >100km.
• Management & Compliance Fee $12/hr covers supervision, WHS, licensing, uniform, incident reporting, insurance ($20M public liability).

6. Security-Specific Obligations (Not in Normal Companies)
• Licensing: We operate under QLD Security Firm Licence + NSW Master Licence + VIC Business Registration etc for national work. Guards hold valid QLD Security Licence subclass + Crowd Control + RSA + First Aid + White Card for construction.
• Subcontracting: For Australia-wide coverage, we may use vetted licensed subcontractors who hold valid Master Licence for that state and have signed NDA.
• Incident Reporting: We provide incident reports, but are not liable for client property loss unless due to our negligence. Client must have own insurance.
• Armed/Bodyguard: Requires risk assessment, client must disclose threats. We may require 2 guards minimum. Category H firearm licence required.
• Right to Refuse: We can refuse or withdraw service if client provides false info, unsafe conditions, or requests unlawful activity.

7. Liability
Liability limited to service fees paid for that shift. Not liable for indirect loss. Client indemnifies us for failure to disclose risks.
